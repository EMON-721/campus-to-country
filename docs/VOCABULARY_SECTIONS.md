# Vocabulary topics and IELTS Skills

The full original list retains all 3,003 entry IDs, headwords, labels, source details and Bangla meanings. The original 1,000-word collection also remains available. Classification is separate in `lib/vocabulary-classification.json`; it does not rewrite source translations or combine homonyms. Source labels distinguish, for example, the financial bank from a river bank. Any existing source-language inaccuracies remain editable through the original learning editor.

The screenshot's 22 category cards are included: Education; The environment; Healthcare; Work; Travel; Science & technology; Crime & the law; Food & diet; Media & the news; Money & finance; Sport & fitness; Art & culture; Family & relationships; Transport & traffic; Housing & home; Business & the economy; Language & communication; Weather & climate; Society & government; Shopping & consumerism; Nature & wildlife; Spelling from listening. Four support categories cover Academic ideas & descriptions; Numbers, time & directions; Core grammar words; Everyday actions & descriptions.

Every source entry has exactly one main category. Related-topic tags may also be retained. Spelling is an overlapping curated practice selection, not a separate imported recording corpus. Counts and levels are calculated from currently available entries and filters; screenshot counts (34 words/B1 or 46 spelling words) are not invented. Existing source CEFR strings can contain multiple levels, and absent levels remain unlisted. Topic/skill groupings are study organisation, not official exam-word classifications.

Learners can search, filter by category/skill/source level/progress, reveal Bangla meanings, save a review, quiz only the current selection, mark new/learning/learned and reset progress. Signed-in progress is owner-scoped in the existing D1 `records` table under `vocabulary-progress`; guest progress stays in browser storage. Atomic JSON-path updates preserve other words. Existing account deletion already removes owner records, including this progress. The explicit progress API allows MFA administrators to review/correct/reset selected applicants with a reason and audit event. It does not send email or change account authorization.

Admin → IELTS → Vocabulary offers category editing, the full learning editor with per-word primary topic/related topics/skill/spelling fields, plus applicant progress controls. Category cards can be reordered/hidden/added, while original word editing/hiding/restoring remains in the learning API. Custom words without metadata default to cross-topic everyday language. Primary category IDs and skills are validated independently of word text.

`/ielts/skills` and the retained `/ielts/lessons` present the existing 28 units grouped into Listening (8), Reading (8), Writing (8), Speaking (4). Cards count actual available exercises (currently one per seed unit), not the reference image's larger external exercise counts. Starting a unit opens its existing strategy, context, exercise and review. Reviewed status uses loaded personal study history; it is not a band score or a mastery claim. Optional General Training units retain their labels. Vocabulary and skill pages link to one another using the skill filter.
